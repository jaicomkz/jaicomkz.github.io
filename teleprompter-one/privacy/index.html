Teleprompter One

Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Summary

Teleprompter One does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only to record videos on your iPhone while you read your script. Nothing is streamed or uploaded.

Photos

The app asks to add videos to Photos only when you save a video. It cannot read your photo library.

Your scripts and videos

Scripts and recorded takes are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. Scripts may be included in your own device backups; recorded takes are excluded from backups.

Network

The app connects to Apple for App Store purchases. It also reads the current time from apple.com to keep the free export timer accurate. These requests contain no personal data, identifiers or cookies.

Purchases

Subscriptions and offer codes are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Sharing

Videos and scripts leave your iPhone only when you choose Share. The app you share to handles them under its own policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
